Title: kmscon added to the moonbase

KMSCON is a DRM/KMS userspace terminal emulator. This local Lunar module
12 applies Sam's four ordered clipboard patches to upstream KMSCON 10.1.2, builds
13 the native X11/Wayland user-session peers, and provides optional, manual-only
14 dinit service templates. No Xvfb, systemd, libseat, D-Bus or accelerated OpenGL
15 backend is required by the selected DRM2D profile.
