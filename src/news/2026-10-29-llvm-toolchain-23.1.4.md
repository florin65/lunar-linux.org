Title: LLVM toolchain updated to 23.1.4

The Lunar Linux LLVM toolchain has been updated to version 23.1.4.

The LLVM project is now maintained through separate Lunar modules for `llvm`, `clang`, `lld`, `compiler-rt` and `openmp`.

All the toolchain modules were moved to the compilers/llvm-stack directory.
